Kennismigrant Visa — Step by Step Process
1

Receive & Sign Job Offer Letter

The offer must be from an IND-recognised sponsor. Salary must be at or above the Kennismigrant threshold (€5,688/mo gross for 30+). Verify before signing.

Timeline: Week 0
2

Employer Submits IND Application

Your employer's HR submits the Kennismigrant application via the IND online portal (IND's Business portal). You provide: passport copy, diploma/degree, employment contract, recent passport photos.

Timeline: Within 1 week of signing
3

IND Processes Application

IND reviews and approves. For recognised sponsors, processing is typically 2–4 weeks. You'll receive an MVV (Machtiging tot Voorlopig Verblijf) — the entry visa sticker for your passport.

Timeline: 2–4 weeks
4

Collect MVV at Dutch Embassy India

Visit the Dutch Embassy/Consulate in New Delhi or Mumbai (or via VFS) to collect your MVV sticker. Book appointment online well in advance — slots fill up. Bring original documents.

Timeline: 1–2 weeks (appointment dependent)
5

Travel to Netherlands

Fly to Amsterdam (AMS). At border control, show your MVV and employment documents. You're entering to work — be clear and confident with border officers.

Timeline: Book flights after MVV received
6

Collect Residence Permit (IDP) in Netherlands

Within ~3 months of arrival, collect your Verblijfsvergunning (Residence Permit) from the IND Service Counter (IND Loket). This is your actual 1–5 year permit. Your employer coordinates this appointment.

Timeline: Within 90 days of arrival
7

Register at Gemeente + Get BSN

Register at local municipality within 5 days. Receive your BSN (citizen service number). Required for payroll, healthcare, banking. Your employer usually helps book this appointment in advance.

Timeline: Day 1–5 after arrival
