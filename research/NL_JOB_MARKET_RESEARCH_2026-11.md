# Netherlands job-market research for Sai Lakshmi Elusuri

**Prepared:** 25 November 2026
**Targets:** (1) Senior Power BI Developer / Fabric analytics engineer, (2) BI delivery roles (Scrum Master, Product Owner for data, BI lead)
**Method:** Job-board pages (Glassdoor NL, Indeed NL, SimplyHired NL, Harnham, Frank Recruitment Group), salary sites, UWV/CBS-based labour-market summaries, IND and EU immigration pages, community event pages. Postings are a snapshot from aggregator pages dated February to November 2026, so some may have closed. Salaries are what employers posted, not what they paid.

---

## 1. Bottom line

1. **Demand for her skill set is real but cooling.** Aggregator pages showed several hundred Power BI listings in the Netherlands at any time (743 on Glassdoor NL in July 2026; 260 "BI developer"; 114 in Amsterdam in August). UWV counted about 18,800 open ICT vacancies at the end of 2025, falling since early 2023 but still hard to fill. Randstad reports entry-level vacancies more than 30% below 2022; senior profiles are less affected.
2. **Dutch language is the biggest filter, not work authorisation.** Of the Utrecht postings reviewed, most hospital, pension, insurer and public-sector roles are written in Dutch or ask for fluent Dutch (for example GLS Netherlands "BI Developer (Fluent in Dutch)" and a Michael Page healthcare analyst role requiring full Dutch). She lists Dutch at A1. Lead with the English-friendly employers (section 5) and use Dutch-language postings to ask whether the team works in English.
3. **The site's €70K-€90K "market benchmark" is the top of the evidence, not the middle.** Posted ranges for medior-to-senior Power BI roles in Utrecht and Amsterdam mostly sit at €4,200-€6,500 gross per month (about €54K-€84K a year with the 8% holiday allowance). Banks and consultancies reach €6,000-€8,700 a month. A realistic ask for a senior profile with Fabric skills is roughly €5,500-€7,000 a month, with the upper end needing a bank, consultancy or strong Fabric match.
4. **Her best-fit niche is Fabric.** Postings that name Microsoft Fabric (Dura Vermeer, REBO Vastgoed, NIBC, ITSync, Quanto, Goudsmit Magnetics, Brigital) match her DP-600 and lakehouse work directly. Pure Power BI report-building roles are more crowded and lower paid.
5. **The delivery track pays similarly but is narrower.** Scrum Master and Product Owner roles in Utrecht post about €3,800-€6,800 a month (Rabobank Data Product Owner up to €8,563). They are mostly Dutch-language, so this track is a second priority unless she finds an English-speaking team.

## 2. Role tracks and search terms

**Track A: Technical (primary).** English: Power BI Developer, Senior BI Developer, BI Engineer, Analytics Engineer, Fabric Engineer, Power BI Consultant, Data & Analytics Consultant, BI Specialist. Dutch: BI-ontwikkelaar, BI-specialist, Power BI ontwikkelaar, data-analist, datawarehouse BI-specialist.

**Track B: Delivery (secondary).** Scrum Master (BI/data), Product Owner Data, Data Product Owner, BI Project Manager / Projectleider BI, Analytics Delivery Lead, Data Platform Product Owner.

**Title advice.** The repo currently uses three names for the delivery target ("Analytics Delivery Lead & BI Programme Manager", "BI Delivery Coordinator", "Delivery Lead"). Dutch employers search "Scrum Master", "Product Owner" and "Projectleider". Pick one headline for LinkedIn and use the others as keywords.

- `("Power BI" OR "PowerBI") AND ("Fabric" OR "Lakehouse") AND (Utrecht OR Amersfoort OR Amsterdam)`

## 6. Recruiters and agencies

- **Frank Recruitment Group (Nigel Frank brand):** specialist Microsoft data and BI recruiter with a Dutch page (nigelfrank.com/nl). Best fit for Power BI and Fabric.
- **Harnham:** specialist data and AI recruiter with an Amsterdam office; listings seen skew to Tableau, Python, Databricks and AWS, with some Power BI. Senior Data Analyst bands seen €80K-€115K.
- **Michael Page Netherlands:** posts Utrecht data analyst roles (one seen at €3,900-€4,800 a month requiring fluent Dutch, so check language first).
- **Hays and Robert Walters (Netherlands):** publish salary guides; useful for benchmarking and for permanent senior roles.
- **Yacht and Brunel:** large Dutch IT staffing brands named in the existing playbook; not independently checked in this research.
- **Computer Futures** was removed from the site's list because I could not confirm a current Dutch BI practice.

## 8. Action plan

**Weeks 1-2**
- Pick the LinkedIn headline (Technical track) and set Open to Work for the Netherlands.
- Register with Frank Recruitment Group, Harnham, Michael Page and Hays; ask each for the budget band and language needs of their open BI roles.
- Apply to 5-8 Tier 1 postings; message the Tier 2 employers' data leads asking about team language.

**Weeks 3-6**
- Join the Dutch Fabric User Group on Meetup and register for the 9 February session if in the Netherlands (or watch recordings).
- Buy a PBIG ticket for 4-6 March (Utrecht).
- Keep a tracker: employer, role, language, posted band, contact, status. Aim for 10 applications a week for the first month.

**Weeks 7-12**
- Use PBIG and Fabric User Group contacts for referrals into Tier 2 and Tier 3 employers.
- For Dutch-language postings that fit, apply with a short English-first cover letter and a plan to reach B1 Dutch.
- Before any signed offer, ask the employer whether the 30% ruling could apply.
