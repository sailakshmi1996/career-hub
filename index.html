FLAGSHIP CASE STUDY · POWER BI & DAX

Enterprise Semantic Layer & DAX Performance Optimization

Business Problem: Executive dashboards suffered from sluggish load times and excessive memory consumption due to flat denormalized models and bi-directional cross-filtering across tables.
Sai's Role & Scope: Lead Power BI Developer across the enterprise sustainability analytics practice.
Technical Decisions: Refactored flat tables into Kimball star schemas; removed bi-directional cross-filtering; tuned DAX measures using KEEPFILTERS, calculation groups, and Tabular Editor; optimized high-cardinality timestamp columns to maximize VertiPaq Storage Engine batch processing.
Result & Measurement: 40% load-time reduction Achieved up to 40% reduction in visual query latency and 35% memory footprint shrink as measured via DAX Studio and VertiPaq Analyzer benchmarks.
LAKEHOUSE & ONELAKE

Direct Lake & Fabric Lakehouse Architecture Transition
