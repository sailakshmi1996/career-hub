🚩 Dutch Job Posting Red-Flag Filter

  • "Dutch is a plus / pre": In corporate tech teams (e.g. Rabobank, bol.com, ING), this genuinely means optional. However, in smaller domestic companies, "pre" often masks an expectation that internal Slack/standups happen in Dutch.
  • "ZZP / Freelance Only": If a posting says *ZZP* (zelfstandige zonder personeel), it is a freelance contract requiring a Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) registration, not a salaried position. Look for *Vast contract* (permanent) or *Detachering / Interim*.
  • Salary Transparency: If a job listing refuses to state a salary scale or CAO scale, it is often below market. Transparent Dutch employers specify the CAO scale (e.g. "Schaal 8 / 9") directly in the vacancy.
  • "36 vs 40 hours": A 36-hour contract is considered full-time in Dutch banking and public sectors (often taken as 4x9 hours, giving you every Friday off!).

⚡ How You Achieved 40% Speedup (VertiPaq Storage Engine Mechanics)

In Dutch technical interviews, you will be asked: "How exactly did you isolate and resolve the bottleneck?" Explain that the VertiPaq engine operates two execution pathways: the Storage Engine (SE), which is multi-threaded and blazing fast on compressed columns, and the Formula Engine (FE), which is single-threaded and slow. Slow reports occur when complex iterators force VertiPaq to generate thousands of callbacks to the Formula Engine.

❌ Slow DAX (Formula Engine Heavy — Callback Lag) 
// Inefficient row-by-row iteration forcing FE execution:
Delayed Sustainability Issues = 
SUMX(
    SustainabilityEvents,
    IF(
        SustainabilityEvents[ResolutionDate] > SustainabilityEvents[TargetDate] 
        && SustainabilityEvents[Severity] = "High",
        SustainabilityEvents[ImpactScore],
        0
    )
)
✔ Optimized DAX (Multi-threaded xmSQL Storage Engine) 
// Pushed directly to VertiPaq Storage Engine (40% Speedup):
Delayed Sustainability Issues = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM(SustainabilityEvents[ImpactScore]),
    KEEPFILTERS(SustainabilityEvents[ResolutionDate] > SustainabilityEvents[TargetDate]),
    SustainabilityEvents[Severity] = "High"
)
Storage Compression Optimization: Moved calculated columns into SQL views and Power Query (M), allowing VertiPaq to build optimal Run-Length Encoding (RLE) bit-packed dictionaries. Sorted the primary fact table by high-cardinality foreign keys prior to ingestion.

🚫 What Recruiters CANNOT Ask Under Dutch Law (AVG)

The Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) and Dutch Equal Treatment laws prohibit employers from requesting the following during interviews:

  • BSN (Citizen Service Number): Employers are legally forbidden from asking for your BSN during interviews. They may only collect your BSN after a formal employment contract is signed for tax administration.
  • Date of Birth / Age: You are not required to provide your date of birth or age on your CV or in screenings to prevent age discrimination.
  • Marital Status, Children, or Pregnancy: Asking whether you are married, have children, or plan a family is strictly illegal under Dutch labor law.
  • Passport / ID Copy Before Offer: Recruiters cannot demand a scan of your passport or residence permit prior to extending a job offer.

✅ What You SHOULD Disclose Legally

To smoothly bypass corporate visa sponsorship screening while remaining fully compliant:

  • Labor Market Status: State that you hold a derived right of residence as a dependent partner: "Arbeid vrij toegestaan, TWV niet vereist".
  • No Employer Costs: Clarify that hiring you incurs zero IND recognized-sponsor fees and zero work permit applications.
  • Location & Commute: State your residence in the Utrecht region and availability for onsite/hybrid work across the corridor.
