"Open to Work" Settings — Get Found by Dutch Recruiters
The most underused LinkedIn feature for international job seekers

⚙️ Exact Settings to Configure

  • Job Titles to Add: Power BI Developer, BI Developer, Data Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst, Azure Data Analyst, Senior BI Developer, Data Visualization Analyst
  • Locations: Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht, The Hague, Eindhoven, Netherlands (Remote)
  • Job Types: Full-time, Contract
  • Visa / Work Authorization: Select "I will need a visa sponsorship" or equivalent — this filters you into recruiter pools specifically looking for visa candidates
  • Visibility: Set to "All LinkedIn members" (not just recruiters) — this adds the green "Open to Work" banner visible to everyone
  • Start Date: Immediately (or within 1 month)
🟢
The Green "Open to Work" Banner Works Profiles with this banner receive 40% more InMail messages from recruiters. Dutch tech recruiters at firms like Yacht, Huxley, and Michael Page actively filter by this flag. Don't hide it.
